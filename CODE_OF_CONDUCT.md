# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Seireth welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience,
or role.

We expect participants to:

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Give and receive constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect the project's authorized-use and security requirements.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized language or attention, publishing private information, and any other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when appropriate.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to
[contact@hstensgaard.com](mailto:contact@hstensgaard.com).

Maintainers will review reports fairly and confidentially, take any action they
consider appropriate, and protect the privacy of the reporter whenever
possible.

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, reviews, and other project-managed channels.
